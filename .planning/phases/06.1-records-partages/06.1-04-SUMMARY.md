# Phase 6.1 Plan 04: Shared records display in the original graphics Summary

The STATS dialog can now show who holds the best time and the player's own best in its original grid, and the RECORDS plaques on the adventure and ending screens can show « TOUS » / « MOI » and detect a click or tap. Nothing is wired yet; 06.1-05 does that. Without records data, every screen produces exactly the R2.0.21 call sequence.

## What was built

**Task 1 — STATS (src/statsdialog.ts)**
- `recordLabel(time, name)` gives « 1:23 (Sophie) », or just the time when the name is null or blank. 06.1-05 writes it into `#result-record` of the vector panel.
- `layoutRecordRow(cells, measure, personalLabel = 'MOI')` is pure:
  - the holder name `(NAME)` is right-aligned on x 489;
  - when the personal best differs, `MOI` goes at x 335 and the personal time 6 px after it;
  - the name keeps an 8 px gap after the personal time. It is shortened on code points with a trailing « . », down to `(S.)` at minimum, and never goes left of x 335.
- `composeStatsDialog` draws `options.record` on the sixth right row only once RECORD is revealed (`revealed ≥ 10`). That row is aligned on COMBO MAX. and is empty in the original. `STATS_ORDER` and `StatsValues` are unchanged.

**Task 2 — RECORDS plaques (src/adventure.ts)**
- `AdventureView.scope` and `EndingView.scope` take `'TOUS' | 'MOI' | null`. Null or empty draws nothing.
- On the adventure screen, `advTexts` draws the scope after the three scores. It uses the plaque font, outlined, 0xffff4c, centred on 548 at baseline 58. Without the pack it falls back to 10 px vector text.
- On the ending screen, `drawEnding` draws the scope after the plaque. It uses the table font in `ADV_ENDING.highlight`, centred on 320 at baseline 164. The run's row stays highlighted and « Score : N » is still drawn.
- `advPlaqueAt` covers (456, 0, 184 × 65) and `endingPlaqueAt` covers (228, 84, 184 × 65). Left and top edges are included, right and bottom excluded, and non-finite coordinates return false.
